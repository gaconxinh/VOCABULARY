Focus the meaning

Cleft sentences: It is/was … that/who …

5 points
Rule: Use It is/was + focused part + that/who + the rest to emphasise one part of a sentence. Use who for a person; that is used for things, time and place in the textbook examples.

1. Focus on the person.

Nam taught Mai how to use the app.

It was ______ taught Mai how to use the app.

2. Focus on the thing.

Nam taught Mai how to use the app.

It was ______ that Nam taught Mai how to use.

3. Focus on the time.

Nam taught Mai how to use the app in the library last weekend.

It was ______ that Nam taught Mai how to use the app in the library.

4. Choose the correct cleft sentence.

Emphasise “20 dollars”: He gets 20 dollars every week by doing chores.

5. Who or that?

“It was John ______ helped his grandpa.”
